About Us

Supplying the UAE's networks, one tested product at a time

Rychlost is a wholesale and retail supplier of structured cabling, fiber, rack hardware, and telecom equipment for integrators, contractors, and IT teams across the UAE.

Our story

We keep structured cabling, fiber, rack enclosures, and connectivity hardware in stock and tested, so an installer or project team doesn't stall waiting on a part. Everything we carry is checked before it leaves our warehouse.

We supply — we don't install. That keeps our focus narrow: sourcing hardware that's proven in the field, keeping it in stock, and getting it to a site on time. Your own team or contractor handles the install.
